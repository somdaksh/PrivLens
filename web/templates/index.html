HOW PRIVYLENS WORKS

A simple, 3-step privacy check
for your data.

Easily find personal data, verify what you find, and spot missing privacy rules in three simple steps.

STEP 01 / ADD YOUR FILES

Upload your spreadsheet or database.

Drop a CSV spreadsheet or connect a SQLite database. PrivyLens inspects your files safely in read-only mode (mode=ro). Your data never leaves your computer.

Open Data Sources
STEP 02 / AUTOMATIC SCAN

Find 5 types of personal data.

PrivyLens scans columns and checks data samples to identify Personal Identity (name, DOB, gender, age), Contact Info (email, phone), Government IDs (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, passport, driving licence), Financial Info (bank accounts, IFSC, card numbers, UPI IDs), and Location (addresses, PIN codes, coordinates).

Explore Detection Rules
STEP 03 / REVIEW & EXPORT

Review findings & export reports.

Check every detected field yourself, make sure each has an approved purpose and retention schedule, easily spot missing details, and generate clean audit reports in PDF or Markdown with one click.
